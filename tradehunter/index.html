Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Posting a job is free, and so is joining and applying as a trade. There are no lead fees, no monthly membership and no commission taken from the job.

How do you check trades?

Every trade uploads photo ID, proof of public liability insurance and any accreditations their work needs, such as Gas Safe or NICEIC. We check them before the profile goes live and again when cover renews.

Where does Trade Hunter work?

We're live across Kingston, Surbiton, Esher and Elmbridge, Richmond, Twickenham, Wimbledon and the towns around them, and opening new areas as more trades join.

Do I have to hire someone who applies?

No. Ask as many questions as you like, compare quotes and only hire when you're happy. If nobody's right, close the job.

Can firms use it to find day-rate help?

Yes. Post the trade you need, the start date, how long it runs and the day rate. Labourers, scaffolders, second fix electricians and plumbers nearby can apply the same day.

Can letting agents use it for a whole portfolio?

Yes. Add each property once, raise jobs against the address and give your team access to the same account. Every quote and completed job stays on record.

How do I pay the trade?

You pay the trade directly for the work, at the price you agreed in the chat. Trade Hunter never adds a booking fee on top.
