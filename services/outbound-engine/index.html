    What it does

    A full outreach team’s day, every day.

    1. 01

      Finds the right companies

      Searches public sources, registers and your own criteria for businesses that fit what you sell, and checks each one is real and trading.

    2. 02

      Researches every one

      Reads their website and news, finds the decision maker and the right address, and notes what is worth saying to them.

    3. 03

      Writes a personal first email

      One email per company, written from what it found, checked before it goes, sent from your own mailbox.

    4. 04

      Follows up on time

      Day three, day seven, day fourteen, each adding something new. Nobody slips through.

    5. 05

      Handles what comes back

      Replies, bounces and out-of-office messages are read and logged. Warm replies land in front of your team the moment they arrive.

    6. 06

      Books the conversations

      Interested replies are answered and moved towards a meeting, with your calendar link, and the call list is ready each morning.

    Built on the same system Opera runs its own outreach with. Sends are paced to protect your mailbox reputation, with consent and opt-out rules built in. Your salespeople keep the discovery calls, the demos and the close.
