    Request a call back

    Leave your number and we will ring you back, usually within the hour, 8am to 6pm.

    Do I pay anything up front?+
    Yes. The call-out charge is paid when you book. It covers the engineer's visit, the diagnosis and the labour to assess the machine and, where possible, carry out the repair. Once it is paid we book the engineer for an agreed date and a three-hour arrival window.
    Does the price include parts?+
    No, and that is deliberate. Most repairs need none. If a part is required, the engineer explains the cost before anything is ordered or fitted and you approve it. Fitting on the return visit carries no extra labour charge.
    What if the machine is not worth repairing?+
    We tell you. If the fault you describe points to a machine that should be replaced, we say so before we come out and refund the booking, and we will happily suggest what to buy instead.
    What does the six-month guarantee cover?+
    Our workmanship. If the same fault comes back within six months of the repair we arrange a return visit to put it right, in line with the guarantee terms. Replacement parts carry their own manufacturer warranty.
    Do you charge more at weekends?+
    No. Saturday and Sunday, 8am to 6pm, cost exactly the same as a weekday.
    Is a steam clean worth it?+
    If the machine smells musty, leaves marks on clothes or the door seal has black spots, yes. A hot wash cannot reach the folds of the seal or the back of the soap drawer. Steam at up to 105°C kills the mould and bacteria there. If the seal itself has perished we show you photos and quote a new one before fitting anything.
    Do you repair other appliances?+
    Yes. Tumble dryers, dishwashers, fridges and freezers, and electric ovens and cookers are handled by our sister company LDAR through the same quote and booking.
    I manage properties. Can my tenants book directly?+
    Better than that. Letting and estate agents get a partner portal where your team books on the tenant's behalf, approves parts and receives one itemised invoice a month. The tenant is texted every step. See the letting agents section above or ask us for a login.
