    CALLBY Command

    Customer app + chauffeur app + operations dashboard

    CALLBY

    A chauffeur for your own car. A vetted professional chauffeur comes to wherever the car is and drives you home in it, so the car never gets left behind after dinner, a night out, a flight or a day when driving is the last thing you want to do. Book now, schedule ahead or by the hour, watch the chauffeur arrive on the map and track the whole journey to your door. Chauffeurs run their work from their own app, and CALLBY Command gives the operations team dispatch, live bookings, pricing, incidents and driver applications on one screen.

    Built for CALLBY by Opera Solutions: the customer app for iPhone and Android, the chauffeur app, CALLBY Command for the operations team, the API behind all three and the website at callby.app.

    • Book now, schedule ahead or by the hour
    • Live tracking from booking to your door
    • Favourite chauffeurs and your garage of cars
    • Chauffeur app with jobs, earnings and documents
    • CALLBY Command: dispatch, bookings, pricing, incidents
    • Identity-checked and background-checked chauffeurs
    Visit callby.app (opens in a new tab)

    Client app + coach app + website

    One Way Fit

    Swipe. Match. Train. A swipe-to-book personal coach marketplace. Clients swipe through verified coaches nearby, book a session online or at a gym, park or home, and check in with a four-digit code so the session starts when both arrive. Coaches run their business from their own side of the app, and the operations portal handles verification, sessions, payouts and reviews.

    Designed and built by Opera Solutions with the One Way Fit founders.

    • Swipe to discover verified coaches nearby, with rating, distance, languages, expertise and price on every card
    • Book a session by date and time, online or on site at a gym, park or home, with duration and price agreed up front
    • Check-in codes: the client's four-digit code is entered by the coach so a session only starts when both are there
    • Messages, favourite coaches, streaks and loyalty points, plus One AI, a personal workout assistant subscription
    • Coach side: Today, Sessions, Requests, Clients, Earnings and Calendar, availability, client notes and success stories
    • Coach verification with documents submitted for approval, and earnings split into paid, pending and paid in person
    Visit onewayfit.app (opens in a new tab)

    Customer app + driver mode + business portal

    Clearby

    Book a rubbish or scrap metal collection the way you book a ride. Customers see the price before they confirm and watch the van on the map, drivers run their day from the same app, and trade customers manage sites, collections and invoices from a desk-side portal. Opera Solutions built all three, and the map tiles the app draws.

    Built for Surrey Waste by Opera Solutions: the customer app built for iPhone and Android with Expo, the driver side, the Clearby for Business portal and the tile server that draws its own green map.

    • Map home screen with Surrey Waste vans on the map and three ways to book: rubbish, scrap metal or both in one visit
    • Booking in three steps with the price shown as you pick a load size, loading included, then photos, pin and postcode, then a day and a two-hour window
    • Tracking from booking to done: the van and its route on the map, a status timeline, driver card with call and message, receipt and waste transfer note
    • Weigh & Pay for scrap: pick the metals, give rough weights, see an indicative range, weighed on the van's scales and paid by bank transfer
    • Driver mode: today's run in slot order, before and after photos, kerbside weights and grades, Accept, Set off, Arrived, Issue transfer note and Mark done
    • Clearby for Business: sites, collections, invoices, Weigh & Pay statements, team roles with approval limits and a map of the vans
    Try the app at surreywaste.com/app (opens in a new tab)Clearby for Business portal (opens in a new tab)

    Mobile app + operations dashboard + API

    Nabor

    A London safety and disruption app. Live official updates from Transport for London, the Environment Agency and the Met Office sit alongside community reports a moderator has reviewed, on one map and feed, with alert zones, push notifications, a Nabor Plus subscription and moderated live video.

    Designed and built by Opera Solutions for Nabor: the app, the API and live feed ingestion, the operations dashboard, the moderation console and the website at nabor.app.

    • Live official feeds: TfL line status and road disruptions, Environment Agency flood warnings and Met Office weather warnings, updated in place and resolved when the source drops them
    • Map and feed with the source and status of every item labelled: official source, confirmed, community reviewed or awaiting review
    • Community reports with an optional photo, location rounded to about 100 m, every report held for a moderator before it is shown
    • Alert zones with push notifications, quiet hours and critical alerts that always break through
    • Nabor Plus subscription through Apple in-app purchase, verified on the server, with restore and account deletion built in
    • Live video on LiveKit: eligible accounts can go live from the report screen and moderators see every stream in the console
    Visit nabor.app (opens in a new tab)

    iOS app for the fitness industry

    The Fitness Directory

    Your fitness network. The Fitness Directory connects fitness professionals with gyms, studios and fitness businesses: instructors and coaches build a verified profile and find work, and operators post roles, fill shifts and find qualified people, all in one app.

    • Community feed where professionals share wins, pass on leads and cover each other's classes
    • Directory of verified professionals with specialities, location and reviews
    • Jobs and shifts: operators post roles and the app matches them with verified professionals
    • Verified profiles with photo ID, insurance and first aid checks and their expiry dates
    • Business side for gyms and studios: manage roles, find talent and grow the team
    • Founding Member and Pro membership built in
