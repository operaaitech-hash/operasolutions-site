    What we do

    The harbour decides
    what’s for supper.

    We buy what comes off the day boats, then cook it simply over hardwood coals. Some days that means turbot with sharp butter. Others, a big pan of mussels and a table full of friends.

    We are family-run, properly local and happiest when the room is loud. Come hungry. Order another bottle. Stay for pudding.

    See what’s cooking

    The old hearth

    Nothing clever.
    Just the right heat.

    Our hearth is the centre of the room. We cook over oak and ash because smoke, blister and a little char make beautiful fish taste more like itself.
